Each pair is one ramp and what a greyscale print keeps of it. The number that decides whether something reads as a rainbow is how far around the hue wheel it travels. Two of the restricted ramps hold a wider minimum luminance gap than the full sweep does, which means they are better ordinal scales as well as quieter ones.
