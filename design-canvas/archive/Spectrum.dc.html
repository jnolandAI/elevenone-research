What the two laws cost

Photopic lightness rises into the green and falls again, so its six samples are not ordered by value: the closest pair sits 0.018 apart in luminance and collapses in a greyscale print.

The ordinal law descends steadily and holds a minimum gap of 0.049, nearly three times as much. It gives up the physical story to stay readable.

What stays greyscale

Every rule, border and divider. Every label, axis and tick. Every panel and every ground. Body and display text throughout. The claim block and the load path. The mark, in all four cuts.

Colour is a field and one action. Emphasis is still weight.
