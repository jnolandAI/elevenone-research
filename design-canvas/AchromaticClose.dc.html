Notes on the data

The source of record is the SEC frames API. Nothing in this piece is modelled and nothing is estimated beyond the density curves.

METHOD

Every filer with both Revenues and GrossProfit tagged in the SEC XBRL frames API for CY2024 annual periods. Margin is gross profit over revenue, as reported, with no restatement and no normalisation. Cohorts are six equal-count buckets on revenue.

EXCLUDED

221 registrants reported one field and not the other, or reported a revenue of zero. They are dropped. They are not random with respect to margin and we have not tested how far that biases the result.

DENSITY

Gaussian kernel density estimate, Silverman bandwidth 0.046, evaluated on a fixed grid from -5% to 100% so the six cohorts are directly comparable.
