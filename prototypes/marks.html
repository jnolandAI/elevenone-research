Four marks drawn from the systemrather than invented beside it

The previous four directions were drawn in Spectral, Fragment Mono and a yellow accent, against a drafting register that has since been dropped. This set uses only what the brand actually committed to: greyscale, Familjen Grotesk, and the halftone screen at its own constants.

The directions

Each shown at display size, in the horizontal lockup, and at the sizes a favicon and an avatar actually get. Where a mark needs a separate small-size cut, that cut is shown rather than hidden.

What each one costs. A mark is a maintenance commitment, not a one-time drawing. It has to survive a favicon, a social avatar, a report cover, an email signature, and a partner's slide at 30% scale. Anything that needs a different drawing at each of those is four marks, not one.

The halftone constants used here are the engine's own: staggered lattice, 15 degree screen angle, dot area carrying the value. Nothing new was invented for these, which is the point. Ink is #131312 throughout and no direction uses a second value that is not a dot.
