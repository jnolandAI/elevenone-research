Five palettes, taken from your gallery

Three of these are your own saved cards adopted verbatim, hex for hex. Two are derived from other items you saved. All five keep the structure your cards actually use: a very dark anchor, a light anchor, then named saturated secondaries out of earth and nature.

Sequential, diverging and categorical ramps are derived from each set by rule rather than by taste, so the comparison is about whether a named brand palette can carry real data honestly. Charts are the same 2,186 SEC registrants, CY2024.

One conflict to resolve. Your light anchors are Bone #F2EFEA and Sand Dune #EADBC8, which are warm off-white and cream. You told me earlier the substrate must never be off-white, cream or paper toned. Your saved palettes and your stated constraint disagree, and I have rendered these on their native warm grounds rather than quietly neutralising them. If the constraint still holds, every one of these needs its light anchor swapped for a neutral, which changes them considerably.

Swatch names are set in Anton for this sheet only, to match the Digital Trade card format. It is not a typeface proposal. CMYK is omitted deliberately: those values belong to a printer profile, not to me.
