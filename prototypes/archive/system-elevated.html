What changed in this pass. Every ramp is now computed in OKLCH rather than sRGB, so brass never passes through khaki on its way to grey and the neutrals never drift warm. Glass is no longer a transparent rectangle: it carries a masked gradient hairline that is bright at the top left and fades to a dark contact edge at the bottom right, an inner top highlight, saturation and brightness lift on the backdrop, and a specular that tracks your pointer. Three glass variants exist, clear, tinted and deep, and the deep one inverts the whole treatment.

Also added. A layered radial mesh under the page with film grain over it to kill gradient banding, a brass gradient running through the display type, and a four step shadow scale with a warm cast rather than a neutral one.

Unchanged. Neutral grey, brass, one accent per surface, colour only ever encoding, and every figure drawn from the same 2,186 SEC registrants.
