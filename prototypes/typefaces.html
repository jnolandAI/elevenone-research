Every specimen below uses real copy and real figures from Brief 001. Sizes are corrected for x-height, so the faces are compared at the same optical size rather than the same nominal one. The correction factor is printed on each card.

The display test

The brief title at 46px and -0.036em tracking, which is where the system runs its display. A face that loosens up or collides at this tracking is out, whatever else it does well.

The reading test

The opening of Brief 001 at 17px and 1.76 line height, at the real measure. This is where most of a reader's time actually goes.

The numerals test

The one that matters most here. Figures inside a sentence must sit in the text without breaking it, and standalone values must align in a column. Both are shown, in the pairing's own text face and its mono.

Brief 001 · Cross-sector financial analysis

Reported gross margin is not one distributionThe peer median sits between two of them

We pulled every SEC registrant that reported both revenue and gross profit for calendar 2024. The median is 38.8%. About one company in six sits within five points of it, and almost exactly as many report above 70%.

Published 6 August 2026 4 min read Universe 2,186 registrants

The spread swamps the middle

A benchmark is only useful when the distribution around it is tight enough that distance from it means something. This one is not. Half the universe reports a gross margin between 23.1% and 59.7%, and about one company in six sits within five points of the 38.8% median. Eight companies in ten fall in a band 64 points wide.

Light track is the tenth to ninetieth percentile, the darker band is the interquartile range, the dot is the median.
