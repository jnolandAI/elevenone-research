## 7. What the figures are, and are not

The service returns each figure as its publisher published it, with a citation that lets you
check it. Publishers revise their data, and a figure may change after it is retrieved; the
citation gives the date it was retrieved. Where the service computes a figure, such as a margin,
it labels the figure as computed and names the reported figures it came from. The service can
still be wrong: a source can be misread, a series misidentified, or a value out of date.

Check a figure against its cited source before relying on it for a decision. Nothing the service
returns is investment, legal, tax, accounting or other professional advice.
